Prototype 2 of 5 · The revenue path

The buyer cannot tell what it costs,
or whether it is even for sale.

The database already stores price, currency, negotiable and status for every listing — the viewer fetches all four and then reads none of them. The price a buyer sees is seller free text, painted 15 px inside a canvas that gets scaled down to 0.35 on a phone. That is roughly 5 px tall, and on a landscape phone there is no native layout to fall back to. A sold listing looks identical to an available one.

Frames below are live DOM, scaled to fit the page — the Today frames reproduce the real fitter's 0.35 floor, so you can see the same shrink that a real buyer gets.

What changed, and what it costs

Problem on stagingProposedEffort
Price is seller free text at canvas scale — ~5 px on a phoneStructured price/currency/negotiable in a hero row at page scale, repeated in a sticky mobile action barS — the columns already exist; read them
status fetched, never checked — a sold card looks liveStatus badge in the hero; sold cards dim the price and strike it through, and the contact button relabels to Sold — no longer availableS
No contact path at all unless the seller's free-text URL is valid http(s)Contact always renders; if the link is invalid the button opens a seller-contact prompt instead of vanishingS
#viewSwitch and the catalog pager disappear below 700 pxLayout switch and pager live in the sticky bar at every widthS
Price sits in one of three equal flex:0 spans — the most ellipsis-prone element on the pagePrice owns its own row and is never truncatedS
0.35 scale floor with no zoom — a phone buyer cannot read anythingFit / 100% / 200% / open-full control, plus the native stacked layout under 700 pxM
One action, not two. There is no make-an-offer flow and no messages or orders table in the schema, so Contact seller is the only buyer action. That is also the honest state: an offer button with nowhere to send the offer is worse than no offer button. The one thing that does need fixing is that contact currently disappears entirely when the seller's free-text URL is not valid http(s) — it should always render and fall back to a contact prompt.
