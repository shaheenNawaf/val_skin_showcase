# Positioning & Branding

**Recommended name:** CardForge (already in the UI, short, game-agnostic)
Alternatives: VaultCard · LootCard · TrophyCase · ShowcaseKit
**Tagline:** "Build. Verify. Sell. — inventory showcase cards for any game."

## Rules
- No game publisher names/trademarks in product name, logo, titles, meta tags, ads.
- Valorant ships as "Pack #1 / template", not as the product identity.
- No bundled publisher assets; catalog synced from public community API.
- Footer disclaimer on every page:
  "CardForge is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Riot Games
  or any game publisher. All game names, skin names and imagery are trademarks/
  property of their respective owners."
- Wider client pool: CS2 cases, MMO inventories, TCG collections later.
