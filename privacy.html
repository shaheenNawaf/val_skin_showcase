Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. The short version

CardForge has no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no ads. We store what you publish and nothing else. Import tokens pass through in memory and are never stored. Published listings are public by design — anyone with the link can see them.

2. What we collect

  • Published listings — the card content you choose to publish: texts, skin picks, ranks, uploaded images, and the share link.
  • View counts — a simple counter per listing, plus a live "viewing now" presence ping while a page is open.
  • Edit tokens — the proof-of-ownership token for editing a listing is stored only in your own browser; our database keeps only its cryptographic hash.

3. What we never collect

  • No accounts, emails, or passwords.
  • No Riot credentials — the import feature processes your access token transiently to read your own data and never stores or logs it.
  • No tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

4. Where your data lives

Listing data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database (Singapore region). The site itself is served by Netlify. Drafts (unpublished cards) never leave your browser — they live in localStorage.

5. Third-party requests your browser makes

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and game item images at runtime from the community-run valorant-api.com. These requests come from your browser directly to those services.

6. Retention and deletion

Listings stay until removed. To delete one, contact us with the listing link (and, if possible, its edit token) and we will remove it. Clearing your browser storage deletes all drafts and edit tokens on your side.
