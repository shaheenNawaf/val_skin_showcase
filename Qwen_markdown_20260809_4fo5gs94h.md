## 1. Acceptable Use
Client shall use the Service solely to showcase digital items the Client lawfully
owns or is authorized to display. Client shall not (a) misrepresent ownership,
origin, or authenticity of any item or account; (b) publish listings that are
fraudulent, misleading, or infringe third-party rights; (c) use the Service to
violate any end-user or terms-of-service agreement of any game publisher.
All transactions, payments, and transfers between Client and third parties occur
entirely outside the Service; Provider does not facilitate, process, escrow, or
guarantee any transaction and is not a party to it. Client is solely responsible
for the legality and fulfillment of such transactions.

## 2. Intellectual Property & Indemnification
Client retains responsibility for all content uploaded, published, or shared
through the Service ("Client Content"), including any game-related imagery and
data retrieved at Client's direction. Third-party game names, item names, and
imagery remain the property of their respective owners and are used subject to
those owners' policies. Client shall indemnify and hold Provider harmless from
any claim, demand, or loss arising from Client Content, Client's use of the
Service, or Client's off-platform transactions. Provider may suspend or remove
any listing upon reasonable notice of abuse, infringement, or publisher
enforcement action. Provider's tools, code, and branding remain Provider's
exclusive property; Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable license
for the subscription term.
